Jaitra - Plan Your Dream Journey Effortlessly
Overview
Jaitra is a premium, beautifully designed travel management application that simplifies the entire lifecycle of a trip. From discovering destinations to packing, tracking daily activities, and final trip feedback, Jaitra acts as your ultimate intelligent travel command center.

Core Workflow & Features
1. Secure Authentication & Dashboard Access
Users can securely sign up or log in to access their personalized travel profile.
Upon entering, users are greeted by a dynamic, time-aware Welcome Command Center displaying their global travel stats (total budget utilized, destinations visited, etc.).
2. Immersive Exploration
A visually stunning "Explore" tab allows users to browse top global destinations grouped by season (Spring, Winter) or style (Beaches).
Destinations feature gorgeous high-quality photography, estimated costs, and top attractions. Selecting a destination instantly begins the trip-planning sequence.
3. Advanced 4-Step Trip Creation Wizard
A seamless, interactive flow that gathers all necessary trip details:

Step 1 (Demographics): Users define their travel type (Solo, Family, Business, Friends). The app dynamically adjusts: asking for full group names and luggage weights for groups, or implementing specialized security/gender inputs for solo travelers.
Step 2 (Schedule): Establishes travel dates and initializes the itinerary timeline.
Step 3 (Smart Checklist): Automatically generates a tailored luggage and parcel packing list based on the group's demographics and inputted luggage weight.
Step 4 (Checkout): Features a high-end, glassmorphic secure payment simulation interface to finalize the booking and lock in the trip.
4. Interactive Itinerary & Package Tracking
Once a trip is booked, users gain access to a dedicated Trip Details dashboard:

Timeline Builder: Users can actively add, arrange, and delete distinct stops along their journey.
Activity Tracking: Within each stop, travelers can schedule activities (with cost estimations) and check them off in real-time as they complete them.
Package Checkbox System: A dedicated parcel/luggage history tab allows users to physically check off their bags and checklist items to ensure nothing is lost during transit.
5. Post-Trip Completion & Feedback Engine
To finalize a journey, users must go through the Jaitra Completion Flow:

Missed Activity Audit: When clicking "Complete Trip", the app intelligently scans the itinerary. If the user missed any planned activities, a prompt forces them to submit a comment explaining why (e.g., "Bad weather", "Ran out of time") before proceeding.
Review System: Users must leave a 1 to 5-star rating and a written review of the trip experience.
Success Lock-in: Once feedback is submitted, a celebration animation locks the trip as "Completed" and saves the memories to their permanent history.
Technology Stack
Frontend: React, React Router DOM
Styling & UI: Tailwind CSS, Custom CSS Glassmorphism
Animations: Framer Motion (for fluid page transitions and curved loop effects)
Icons & Assets: Lucide React
